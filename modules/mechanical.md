# Mechanical Design (high)

Frame, mounting, vibration.

- **Stack:** FC soft-mounted (rubber grommets / gel). Hard-mounted gyros saturate from motor vibration.
- **IMU placement:** frame center, away from motors, on the soft mount — never on the power stage.
- **Connectors:** locked or glued. Vibration undoes friction-fit connectors in flight.
- **Frame:** carbon plates, standoffs, motor mounts. A crash bends arms before it breaks the stack — design the stack to survive the arm bending.
- **Keep-outs:** no components under the battery pad area; no tall parts where the frame clamps the stack.
- **Mechanical exports:** `.step` / `.stl` for frame, mounts and enclosures (see SKILL.md §16).
